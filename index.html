Questions

Still unsure? Ask Mr. Brennan in Room 162 or on GChat.

Do I need an idea to apply?

No. Tell us a problem you care about and choose "Open to a team." Sprints are built for figuring out the idea.

Can I apply with a team?

Yes. Choose "Team," list your teammates, and have each teammate submit their own application.

How are the 40 chosen?

We look at how clearly you describe a problem, why you want to do this, and whether you can attend all six sprints.

What if I miss a sprint?

Life happens. Let Mr. Brennan know ahead of time. Missing more than one may give your spot to someone on the waitlist.

Can I do Patriot Pitch without the sprints?

Yes. Pitch is open to everyone, with no cap. Sprints just give you a head start.

When do I pay the $75?

Only after you're accepted. You'll get a Veracross link on December 1, and payment is due December 8.

Can my family come watch?

Yes. Parents are invited to the Patriot Pitch semifinals on February 20 in the Wagner Entrepreneurial Center.

I didn't get a spot. Now what?

You're on the waitlist, and spots open up. You can still apply to Patriot Pitch by January 31.
