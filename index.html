Questions

Still unsure? Ask Mr. Brennan in Room 162 or on GChat.

Do I have to do the Design Sprints to get into Patriot Pitch?

No. The sprints are not mandatory, and you can be accepted to Patriot Pitch without them. They do help you build and test your idea so your application is stronger.

How many teams get into Patriot Pitch?

24 teams are accepted to the semifinals on February 20. The top 10 advance to Finals on March 10 at the University of Tampa.

I’ve pitched before. Can I use my old pitch?

No. Returning students are welcome to apply, but you’ll need a new pitch for this year’s Patriot Pitch.

Does Patriot Pitch cost anything?

No. There’s no application fee for Patriot Pitch. The $75 fee is only for Design Sprints, and only if you’re accepted.

What are the prizes?

$3,500 in prizes, awarded at Finals on March 10.

Do I need an idea to apply for the sprints?

No. Tell us a problem you care about and choose “Open to a team.” Sprints are built for figuring out the idea.

How are the 40 sprint spots chosen?

We look at how clearly you describe a problem, why you want to do this, and whether you can attend all four sprints.

What if I miss a sprint?

Life happens. Let Mr. Brennan know ahead of time. Missing more than one may give your spot to someone on the waitlist.

When do I pay the $75?

Only after you’re accepted to the sprints. You’ll get a Veracross link with your decision on December 19, and payment is due January 6.

Can my family come watch?

Yes. Families are invited to Patriot Pitch Finals on Wednesday, March 10 at the University of Tampa.
